# 📊 Marketing Analytics for E-Commerce

This project is a comprehensive marketing analysis of an Indian e-commerce marketplace, inspired by a capstone project from AnalytixLabs. It explores customer behavior, seller performance, product trends, and payment methods to provide actionable business insights using real-world data from Sep 2016 to Oct 2018.

---

## 🧠 Business Objective

The goal of this project is to measure, manage, and analyze the performance of an e-commerce marketplace. Key objectives include:

- Understand customer acquisition, retention, and behavior
- Identify top-performing products, sellers, and regions
- Evaluate customer satisfaction through reviews
- Segment customers and sellers based on revenue
- Discover product bundling opportunities for cross-selling
- Analyze payment preferences and channels

---

## 📦 Dataset Overview

The project uses the following datasets:

- `CUSTOMERS.csv`: Customer information
- `SELLERS.csv`: Seller profiles
- `PRODUCTS.csv`: Product catalog
- `ORDERS.csv`: Order history
- `ORDER_ITEMS.csv`: Item-level purchase details
- `ORDER_PAYMENTS.csv`: Payment methods and values
- `ORDER_REVIEW_RATINGS.csv`: Customer reviews and ratings
- `GEO_LOCATION.csv`: Location mapping

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 🔹 Exploratory Data Analysis
- Total revenue, product count, categories, locations, and sellers
- Monthly trends in revenue and quantity sold
- Seasonality analysis by month, week, and region
- Top products and categories by month/state
- Acquisition and retention trends over time

### 🔹 Segmentation
- RFM Analysis to group customers by value
- Seller segmentation based on revenue

### 🔹 Customer Satisfaction
- Top and bottom-rated products and categories
- Average rating by product, seller, location, and time

### 🔹 Cross-Selling Analysis
- Top combinations of products frequently bought together

### 🔹 Payment Analysis
- Most preferred payment methods by customers
